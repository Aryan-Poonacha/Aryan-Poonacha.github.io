01 — Latest · October 2026

Live · desktop Chrome

Empire Earth (2001), running in Chrome.

My first real game, at about five. I got the original Windows executable running in a WebAssembly x86 emulator, with DirectDraw and Direct3D 7 reimplemented on WebGPU. Single-player and multiplayer, public and private lobbies, no install. BottleShip did most of the graphics work; I filled in the Win32 pieces Empire Earth needed and sent them upstream.

  1. 01Win32 + message-queue fixes · merged into BottleShip
  2. 02Two-player relay · up to eight over direct WebRTC
  3. 03Saves and game files stay in your browser

Four epochs so far.

Named after the game. Every epoch changed the tools, the country and the problem; the habit of shipping did not.

    First game at five, first novel at fifteen.

  2. II · Kunshan · 2019–2021

    A kid from India, on a Chinese scholarship, at an American university.

    Duke Kunshan University dual degree, B.Sc. Data Science, with a cohort drawn from forty countries.

    Columnist for The Duke Chronicle's Kunshan Report. National finalist, Alibaba GET Global Startup Challenge, for a mental-health startup for Chinese high-schoolers.

  3. III · Durham · 2021–2024

    Research, hackathons, and a master's in AI.

    Data+ dashboard for Durham Public Schools, 3,000+ monthly visits, featured by Duke. Bass Connections fluid simulation of housing inequality, shown in a gallery.

    Best Overall Hack at GeorgiaTech Robotech 2022, then Agronomy VR in the CreateX accelerator. TA for CS 307/308: 40+ students, 200+ code reviews.

    M.Eng. Artificial Intelligence, Duke, 2024.

  4. IV · San Francisco · 2025–

    Founding engineer, twice. Founder next.

    Lightfield (from the team behind Tome): founding AI product engineer, shipping the agentic chat, RAG and tool calling inside the product, daily.

    Tomex: founding AI engineer, owning the agentic chat application, agent sandboxes, an in-house PR review bot and the engineering culture around them.

    October 2026: Empire Earth in the browser. Next: a company, and the next novels.

2026 · systems

Win32 fixes upstream in BottleShip

To run a 2001 game on an emulator that reimplements Windows in the browser, something had to fill the gaps: Visual C++ 6 runtime shims, a Direct3D 7 loader module, a DirectDraw device-context fix that brought the menu art back, a message-queue bug that crashed one quit in three, a WebGPU depth-clear fix, and a regression harness. 02 — Games · 5 pieces · 2017–2026

Old worlds, new runtimes.

The first game I took seriously was Empire Earth, at about five. Since then I have shipped small games on the web and in VR, and this year put the first one back on screen without an install.

2026 · live · desktop Chrome

Empire Earth in the browser

The original 2001 RTS and its expansion, playable in desktop Chrome: single-player, and multiplayer with public and private lobbies over a two-player relay or direct WebRTC for up to eight. It runs the unmodified executable on BottleShip; saves stay in your browser.

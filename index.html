Selected work

  1. 2026

    Empire Earth in the browser

    The original 2001 Windows game, playable in desktop Chrome with no install: single-player, and multiplayer with public and private lobbies. It runs the unmodified executable on BottleShip, an x86 emulator that reimplements Win32 and DirectDraw/Direct3D 7 on WebGPU. I added what Empire Earth needed and the work was merged upstream: VC6-era C-runtime shims, a Direct3D 7 loader module, a DirectDraw fix that restored the menu art, a message-queue bug that crashed one quit in three, a WebGPU depth-clear fix, and a regression harness. The site, game-file delivery and a two-player relay run on Cloudflare Pages and one small Render service; three- to eight-player matches have been played across two machines.
